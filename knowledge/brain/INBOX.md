# INBOX — raw thoughts, unpolished

*Capture first, classify later. This file exists so that a thought does not have to know what it is
before it is allowed to be recorded. Nothing here is scored, sourced, or validated.*

> **The rule for this file: write it wrong rather than not at all.** A half-formed sentence with a date
> beats a well-formed atom you never wrote because you were not sure whether it was a concept or a
> mechanism. Triage is a separate activity from capture, and mixing them is why capture stops happening.

---

## Format

One block per thought. Only the date and the thought are required.

```markdown
### 2026-08-18 · a few words
The thought, however rough. Fragments and questions are fine.
?? what would falsify this
-> where it might land: CONCEPT / IDEA / VERDICT / discard
```

`??` marks the open question, `->` marks a guess at the destination. Both optional — a guess is a
convenience for triage, never a commitment.

## Triage

Sweep periodically. Every entry leaves by one of four doors, and **the fourth door is real**:

| Door | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Concept** | it is machinery — a definition, an identity, a measurement discipline | new note in `concepts/`, cite what prompted it |
| **Mechanism** | it names someone who must trade, and what you would put on | new entry in [`../reports/IDEAS.md`](../reports/IDEAS.md) with honest scores |
| **Verdict** | it records something tried and its outcome | append to [`VERDICTS.md`](VERDICTS.md) |
| **Dropped** | it did not survive contact | move to *Dropped* below **with the reason** |

Record the drop. A thought you rejected and can explain is knowledge; a thought that silently vanished
will be re-had, at full cost, in six months. This is the same reason `IDEAS.md` logs its deliberate
non-merges.

**Triage is not compression.** If an entry needs three atoms, make three. If it needs none but is worth
keeping, leave it here and say why — a permanent resident of this file is allowed.
