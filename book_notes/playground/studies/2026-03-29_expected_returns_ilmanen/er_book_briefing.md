## Author's Central Argument

Expected returns across all asset classes can be decomposed into systematic risk premia that are theoretically grounded, empirically robust, and forward-looking. Investors should harvest these premia systematically rather than relying on market timing or pure alpha. The book unifies equity, bond, FX, commodity, and alternative premia under one framework.

## Reading Protocol (Book — Academic-Practitioner Synthesis)

This book sits between academic research and practitioner application. It is evidence-based but written for practitioners.
- Focus on: how each risk premium is defined, what the empirical evidence shows, and what forward-looking indicators the author recommends
- For each chapter: extract the premium definition, key drivers, historical evidence, and tactical forecasting signals
- Note connections across chapters: how do equity, bond, and FX premia interact? How do behavioral biases create exploitable anomalies?
- Identify which premia are implementable with available local data
