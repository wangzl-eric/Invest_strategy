## Author's Central Argument

Global macro trading is a process-driven, multi-asset discipline that profits by identifying and positioning around macroeconomic themes across equities, fixed income, FX, and commodities. Success requires rigorous trade process, disciplined risk management, and deep understanding of the interplay between central bank policy, economic data, and asset prices.

## Book Structure

### Part One: An Overview of Global Macro (Ch1–6)
- **Ch1:** Global macro landscape — strategy types, return profiles, hedge fund context
- **Ch2:** Trading process — objectivity, position sizing, vol-adjusted sizing, risk/reward, correlation, VaR, stress-testing, Sharpe/Sortino
- **Ch3:** Back-tests, queries, and historical analogs
- **Ch4:** Building blocks — equities, fixed income, FX, commodities and their interrelationships
- **Ch5:** Technical analysis — trends, moving averages, Bollinger Bands, Elliott Wave, oscillators, crowd psychology
- **Ch6:** Systematic trading — framework, factors, risk premia, risk parity

### Part Two: Global Macro Trading Foundation (Ch7–12)
- **Ch7:** Foreign Exchange — USD role, currency regimes, valuation techniques
- **Ch8:** Equities — indices, derivatives, valuation techniques
- **Ch9:** Fixed Income — funding markets, LIBOR/SOFR, swaps, OIS, FRAs, sovereign curves, CDS, ETFs
- **Ch10:** Commodities — supply/demand drivers, contango/backwardation, energy, metals, agriculture
- **Ch11:** Central Banks — monetary policy tools, impossible trinity, QE, ZLB, central bank communication
- **Ch12:** Economic Data — growth, inflation, employment, balance of payments, demographics

## Reading Protocol (Book — Practitioner Guide)

This is a discretionary practitioner's guide, not a quant methods book.
- Focus on: how practitioners think about macro themes, what frameworks they use to relate assets, how they size and manage trades
- For each chapter: extract the mental models and heuristics the author uses, not just definitions
- Note connections between chapters: how does FX regime choice affect fixed income trades? How do central bank tools affect all four asset classes?
- Identify which frameworks are immediately testable with available data vs which require external data
